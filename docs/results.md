## Critical-path optimization evidence

- Behavior-equivalent MESI occupancy implementation (`LEGACY_COUNTONES_OCCUPANCY` passes the same D-cache test): critical period 5437.35 → 171.77 ns (96.8% reduction).

Negative cache/SoC slack is retained to demonstrate why production caches use SRAM macros and why these pre-layout values are not signoff.
